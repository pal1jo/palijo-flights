# Palijo's flight history ✈️

**▶ Open it here: https://pal1jo.github.io/palijo-flights/**

An interactive story of 39 flights between August 2019 and September 2026: 74,084 km, 9 countries and 19 airports.

- Starts on a globe, takes off from Karachi, and follows every flight on a live map
- A fun fact for every flight: the first ever, the longest, the shortest, the biggest day, and more
- Tap the bars at the top to jump to any flight, like chapters in a video
- Ends with the whole web of routes drawn together

Works on phone and desktop, and fully offline: everything is inside one `index.html` file.

Routes are real filed flight plans (Flight Plan Database) and a Flightradar24 track. Aircraft photos are from Wikimedia Commons, credited on each card.
